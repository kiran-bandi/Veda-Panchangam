Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and process your information when you download, install, and use our mobile application Sudoku: Classic & Journey (the "App"), identified on the Google Play Store as com.game.sudoku.bandi.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed as a secure, offline-first application with your data privacy as our highest priority.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Local Storage (Game Progress & Statistics)

All game progress, statistics, level completions, streaks, custom configurations, and achievements are stored strictly locally on your device using a secure SQLite database (Room). This data is never transmitted to, stored on, or accessed by our servers, nor is it shared with any third party.

B. Camera Access

The App includes an optional "Camera Puzzle Scanner" feature allowing you to scan printed Sudoku puzzles from newspapers or books. When you grant camera permission:

  • The camera is used solely to capture and process the layout of the puzzle digits on your screen.
  • Image processing is performed entirely **on your device**.
  • No photos, video streams, or scanned digits are uploaded, shared, or sent to external servers.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party advertising, user tracking, or behavioral analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Facebook SDK). Your gameplay, habits, and preferences remain completely private and secure on your own device.

3. Data Security

We employ standard Android security guidelines to safeguard your locally stored game data. Since we do not transmit or store any personal data on external servers, your gameplay information cannot be accessed externally.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is suitable for players of all ages, including children. We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13, fully complying with COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act) guidelines.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly within this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
