Privacy Policy & Data Security

Effective Date: October 6, 2026

App Name: HisabBook (Personal Interest & Loan Tracker)

🔒 Zero Cloud Data Storage Guarantee:
HisabBook operates strictly on a Local-First & Privacy-First architecture. All your loan records, borrower names, payment histories, and financial data remain 100% on your personal device. We do not operate cloud servers, and we never collect, upload, or sell your financial data.

1. Information We Process

HisabBook processes financial records locally on your Android device to help you calculate and track personal loans, simple interest, and compound interest:

  • Borrower Information: Names, phone numbers, and optional promissory note photos.
  • Loan & Interest Details: Principal amounts, interest rates, calculation methods, start dates, and payment histories.
  • App Preferences: Selected language, security PIN hash, and UPI ID for WhatsApp reminders.

2. How Your Data is Stored

All data entered into HisabBook is stored inside your Android device's private SQLite database (via Room Database Framework) and encrypted DataStore preferences. It is never transmitted across the internet to any remote database.

3. App Permissions & Their Usage

To provide full functionality, HisabBook requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone / Speech Recognition: Used exclusively when you tap the voice input button to speak loan entries. Audio is processed on-device and is never recorded or stored.
  • Photo Picker / Media Access: Used only if you choose to attach a photo of a promissory note or document to a loan record.
  • Phone / WhatsApp Intent Access: Used to launch native phone calls or open WhatsApp when you explicitly click the "Call" or "Reminder" buttons.

4. Data Backup & User Control

Since your financial data remains on your device, you have complete control over your records:

  • Export & Backup: You can export a local JSON backup file anytime from the Settings screen.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete individual loan records, clear app data, or uninstall the app to erase all stored data permanently.

5. Third-Party Services

HisabBook does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks that track your personal financial data.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
