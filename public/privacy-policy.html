Privacy Policy for Veda Panchangam

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

Welcome to Veda Panchangam (వేద పంచాంగం). Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles your information, what permissions are requested, and how your data is protected in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

A. Location Data (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

  • Purpose: Veda Panchangam requests your location strictly to calculate accurate local astronomical events—including Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Moonset, Tithi, Nakshatra, Rahu Kalam, and Yamagandam for your specific city.
  • Usage: Location processing occurs locally on your device in real-time (Foreground only).
  • Storage & Sharing: Your location data is never stored on external servers, never tracked in the background, and never shared or sold to any third parties.

B. Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Purpose: Used strictly to schedule local daily Panchangam summaries and festival alerts on your device.
  • Usage: Notifications are generated locally via Android's AlarmManager. No external notification server stores your data.

C. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

  • We display advertisements served by Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and device identifiers for ad delivery and fraud prevention in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app uses standard Google Services:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Veda Panchangam does not require user accounts or personal profiles. No personal data is stored on remote servers. Uninstalling the app completely clears all local settings and saved bookmarks from your device.

4. Children's Privacy

Veda Panchangam is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. User Permissions Control

You can grant or revoke Location or Notification permissions at any time via your device settings: Settings > Apps > Veda Panchangam > Permissions.
