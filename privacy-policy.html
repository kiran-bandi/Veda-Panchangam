1. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

Welcome to Veda Panchangam (శ్రీ వేద తెలుగు పంచాంగం). Your privacy is paramount to us. This document outlines how our application operates in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

A. Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

  • Purpose: Used strictly to calculate accurate local astronomical events—including Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Moonset, Tithi, Nakshatra, Rahu Kalam, and Yamagandam for your specific location/city.
  • Processing: Location processing is done 100% locally on your device in real-time.
  • Storage: Your location is never stored on remote servers, never tracked in the background, and never shared or sold to third parties.

B. Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

  • Purpose: Used to deliver daily Panchangam updates, auspicious muhurtham reminders, and festival wishes.
  • Processing: Notifications are scheduled locally via Android's native AlarmManager.

C. Advertisements (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob)

  • We display advertisements served by Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to keep the application free for all users. AdMob may process anonymous device identifiers and network state for ad serving and fraud prevention in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.

2. Third-Party Privacy Links

Our app incorporates standard Google SDKs. You can read their privacy practices here:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & Mobile Ads Privacy Policy

3. Data Security & Storage

  • No User Accounts Required: You can use all features of the app without registering or logging in.
  • No External Data Retention: We do not host remote databases containing user identity or personal profiles.
  • Data Removal: Uninstalling the app instantly removes all saved bookmarks, local city choices, and preferences from your phone.

4. Children's Privacy Compliance

Veda Panchangam is designed for general audiences aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Managing Your Permissions

You can grant, deny, or revoke location and notification permissions at any time via your device settings:

Device Settings > Apps > శ్రీ వేద తెలుగు పంచాంగం > Permissions
