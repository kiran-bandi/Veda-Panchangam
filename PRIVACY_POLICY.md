# Privacy Policy for Veda Panchangam (శ్రీ వేద తెలుగు పంచాంగం)

**Effective Date:** September 21, 2026

Welcome to **Veda Panchangam**. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles your information, what permissions are requested, and how your data is protected in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

### A. Location Data (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`, `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`)
- **Purpose:** Veda Panchangam requires your approximate or precise geographical location solely to calculate accurate local astronomical events—including Sunrise, Sunset, Moonrise, Moonset, Tithi, Nakshatra, Rahu Kalam, and Yamagandam for your specific city.
- **Usage:** Location processing occurs **locally on your device in real-time (Foreground only)**. 
- **Storage & Sharing:** Your location data is **never stored on external servers, never tracked in the background, and never shared or sold** to any third parties.

### B. Push Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)
- **Purpose:** Used strictly to schedule and display daily Panchangam summaries, auspicious timings, and festival reminders on your device.
- **Usage:** Notifications are generated locally via Android's AlarmManager. No external server receives notification tokens.

### C. Device Identifiers & Analytics (Advertising)
- **Google AdMob:** We display banner and interstitial advertisements provided by Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers, network state, and app interaction diagnostics to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.

## 2. Third-Party Services
Our app integrates the following standard Google services:
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** For ad monetization. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
Since Veda Panchangam does not require user accounts, passwords, or personal profile creation:
- **No Personal Data Stored:** We do not maintain user databases or store personal identify information on external cloud servers.
- **Data Deletion:** Uninstalling the app completely removes all locally stored preferences (such as saved cities or bookmark lists) from your device.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy
Veda Panchangam is designed for general audiences (Ages 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. User Control & Permissions
You have full control over the permissions granted to Veda Panchangam:
- You can grant or revoke **Location** or **Notification** permissions at any time through your device settings (**Settings > Apps > Veda Panchangam > Permissions**).
- The app will continue to function using default city locations even if location permissions are denied.
